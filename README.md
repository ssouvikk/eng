# Word Power: Vocabulary Through Etymology

A collection of vocabulary notes designed to improve long-term word retention by understanding the structure, origin, and evolution of words.

Each word covers:
- **Word Breakdown:** Prefixes, roots, suffixes, and their meanings.
- **Etymology & Literal Meaning:** Historical origin and original sense.
- **Semantic Evolution:** How the meaning developed into its modern meaning.
- **Common Usage:** Phrases, collocations, and example sentences.
- **Memory Hooks & Word Families:** Techniques for retention and related words.

**Learning Flow:** Components → Literal Meaning → Semantic Evolution → Modern Meaning → Practical Usage.
