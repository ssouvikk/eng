# Word Power: Vocabulary Through Etymology

## Overview
This folder contains vocabulary notes designed to improve **word retention, conceptual clarity, and practical usage** by breaking words into their components and tracing how their meanings evolved over time.

## Learning Approach
Each word is explored through the following steps:

1. **Word Breakdown** — Identify prefixes, roots, and suffixes, along with the meaning of each component.
2. **Etymological Origin** — Understand the word's historical roots and original meaning.
3. **Literal Meaning** — Reconstruct the word's basic meaning from its components.
4. **Semantic Evolution** — Trace how the literal or historical meaning developed into its modern sense.
5. **Modern Meaning** — Understand the word's current meaning and nuances.
6. **Common Usage** — Learn common phrases, collocations, and example sentences.
7. **Memory Hook** — Use a simple mental image or association to make the word easier to remember.
8. **Word Family** — Explore related words, derivatives, and other words sharing the same historical root.
9. **Contextual Application** — Practise using the vocabulary in formal writing, especially UPSC answers where relevant.

## Objective
The goal is to move beyond rote memorisation and understand the **logic behind a word's meaning**:

**Components → Literal Meaning → Semantic Evolution → Modern Meaning → Practical Usage**

This approach helps build long-term vocabulary retention and enables accurate, context-aware use of words in everyday communication, reading, and analytical writing.

## Example
For *conjecture*:

`con- (together) + ject (throw) → throw clues together → form a tentative explanation`

The original metaphor helps explain the modern meaning: a belief or explanation formed from incomplete evidence.

## Intended Use
Use these notes for regular vocabulary revision, active recall, and improving precision in English expression and UPSC Mains answer writing.
